Volunteer Testing — 21-Day Calendar

Test the Calendar

Use it for 21 days. Tell me what felt accurate and what did not. That is the whole ask.

Before you begin

This is a voluntary testing phase for the 21-day behavioral eating pattern calendar. Participation is anonymous. No identifying information is collected or stored.

By downloading the calendar you are agreeing to use it and optionally share feedback after 21 days. You can withdraw at any time. Data collected — pattern codes and feedback responses — is used for instrument validation and will inform future versions of the tool.

This instrument is a behavioral self-report tool. It is not a clinical screener, a diagnostic tool, or a medical assessment.

What you are doing

  • 01 Take the Behavioral Eating Pattern Inventory — or enter your code below if you already have one.
  • 02 Download your pattern calendar. Free. No email required.
  • 03 Use it for 21 days — or as many as you can manage.
  • 04 Come back and retake the inventory. Enter your original code to see what changed.
  • 05 Answer four feedback questions. Optional but useful.
